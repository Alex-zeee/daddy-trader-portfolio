Privacy Policy

Daddy Trader (M. Ali Alvi) · Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Daddy Trader app and website (including the Alexa assistant, the Fundamental page and the BackBar backtesting page) use, and why. We keep it simple: we do not create user accounts, we do not sell data, and we do not run advertising trackers.

1. Microphone and voice

Alexa can listen when you tap the logo or the mic button. The app asks for microphone permission first, and only records while it shows that it is listening. The short recording is sent to Google's Gemini service to turn your speech into text and to prepare the answer. Recordings are not stored by Daddy Trader.

2. Questions and answers

The text of your question is sent to Google Gemini to generate Alexa's reply, and the reply may be turned into speech by Google. Your recent conversation with Alexa is saved only in your own browser/app storage on your device so the conversation can continue. You can clear it at any time with the "new conversation" button, or by clearing the app's data.

3. Market data

To show prices and analysis, the app requests public market data from Daddy Trader's own data files and from public price services (for example gold-api.com for gold and silver, Binance for crypto prices, and open.er-api.com for currency rates). These requests do not include your personal information.

4. Backtesting and settings

Charts, drawings and settings you use on the BackBar backtesting page are saved on your own device. Notifications for signals or news are only sent if you choose to subscribe to them.

5. Social media posting (Facebook, WhatsApp)

Daddy Trader uses an automatic publishing tool to post its own trading signal cards, market analysis and motivation images to Daddy Trader's own Facebook page and WhatsApp channel. For this, Daddy Trader connects only its own accounts through the official services (Meta Graph API and Whapi.Cloud), and the access tokens are kept as private secrets that are never shown or shared. The tool does not collect, store or sell any information about other Facebook or WhatsApp users.

6. Third-party services

  • Google Gemini (speech-to-text, answers, text-to-speech) — Google Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages (hosting this app and website) — GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Meta (Facebook page publishing) — Meta Privacy Policy
  • Whapi.Cloud (WhatsApp channel messages)
  • Public market data services listed above

7. Children

This app is about financial markets and trading and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Not financial advice

Analysis, levels and signals shown by the app are for education and information. Trading involves risk. See also our Terms of Service.
